BaseMatch is a simple app built for recording amateur baseball and softball games.

■ Record every game
Enter the opponent, score, venue, and number of innings. Games are organized on a calendar so you can see at a glance when and where you played.

■ Log at-bats with one tap
Single, double, triple, home run, strikeout, ground out, fly out, double play, walk, hit by pitch, error — 15 outcomes, all one tap away. Innings and RBIs are recorded too.

■ Detailed pitching stats
Innings pitched are entered by outs, so fractional innings like "1.1" are recorded accurately. Track runs, earned runs, hits allowed, walks, strikeouts, and home runs allowed.

■ Stats calculated automatically
From your records, BaseMatch computes:
• Batting Average (AVG)
• On-Base Percentage (OBP)
• Slugging Percentage (SLG)
• OPS
• Earned Run Average (ERA)
• WHIP

Stats are grouped by player, so you can manage your whole team. Filter by all-time or by month.

■ No account, fully offline
No sign-up required. All data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. Works perfectly at fields with no signal.

■ Built for
• Team scorekeepers
• Players tracking their own numbers
• Anyone managing team-wide stats
• Those moving on from paper scorebooks

Available in English and Japanese.
